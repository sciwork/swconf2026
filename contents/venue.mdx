# Venue

The sciwork conference 2026 will take place in **Room 402 and Room 403** of the
**Pioneer International R&D Building**, National Taipei University of
Technology (NTUT).

- **Address**: No. 46, Sec. 3, Zhongxiao E. Rd., Da'an Dist., Taipei City,
  Taiwan
- **Map**: [Google Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nv5JJGcdJP9nuKjq5)

### By MRT

Take the Taipei Metro to **Zhongxiao Xinsheng Station**. Leave the station
from **Exit 3** or **Exit 4**. Walk east along Zhongxiao E. Rd. Sec. 3 to the
Pioneer International R&D Building.

### By Car

Drive to No. 46, Sec. 3, Zhongxiao E. Rd., Da'an Dist., Taipei City. The
Pioneer International R&D Building is on the south side of Zhongxiao E. Rd.
Sec. 3, opposite the NTUT main gate.

### Parking

The Pioneer International R&D Building has a parking lot. Attendees can park
in the parking lot of the building.
