# Call for Proposals

The sciwork 2026 conference brings together professionals, researchers, and
enthusiasts in scientific computing, software engineering, and the open-source
community. This year's conference will be held as a **physical event** on
**2026/12/5** at **NTUT Pioneer International R&D Building, Room 402 and
Room 403**.

We warmly invite you to submit proposals for talks and hands-on workshops
covering a wide range of topics related to scientific computing, AI agent
applications, system performance, and open-source practices.

## Conference Topics (including but not limited to):

1. **AI Agent Applications & Workflows**

   - **Code Generation & Software Engineering**: Prompt/context engineering,
     agent-driven code synthesis, automated test generation, code refactoring,
     and ensuring correctness/alignment with user intent.
   - **AI Agents in Computer Science & Systems**: Autonomous system diagnosis,
     compiler optimization assistance, automated profiling/performance tuning,
     and AI-assisted firmware/embedded development.
   - **AI Agents for DevOps & Infrastructure**: Autonomous CI/CD
     troubleshooting, log analysis, automated incident response,
     infrastructure-as-code generation, and observability automation.
   - **AI Agents in Scientific Computing & HPC**: Automated numerical modeling,
     workflow orchestrations, simulation data analysis, and domain-specific
     agentic tools.

2. **High-Performance Computing (HPC)**: Parallel computing, GPU/heterogeneous
   acceleration, microarchitecture optimization.

3. **Scientific Computing & Data Science**: Numerical analysis, computational
   physics/chemistry, data pipelines & analytics.

4. **Other Scientific & Technical Open-Source Case Studies**

## Submission Guidelines:

1. **Presentation Types**

   - **Technical talk**: 30 minutes (including Q & A). Focuses on sharing
     technical concepts, case studies, or open-source project insights.
   - **Workshop**: 160 minutes (including break). Interactive, hands-on sessions
     designed to walk participants through practical workflows, tools, or live
     coding exercises.

2. **Proposal Submission Content**

   Submit your proposal via
   [this portal](https://pretalx.sciwork.dev/sw26/submit).

   - **Title**: Provide a clear and concise title for your session.
   - **Abstract**: Write a brief abstract summarizing the core focus and value
     of your talk or workshop.
   - **Description**: Provide additional details (e.g., detailed outline, target
     audience, technical prerequisites, environment setup for workshops, and
     expected key takeaways).

3. **Important Dates**

   - **Submission Deadline**: 2026/09/16
   - **Acceptance/Rejection Notification**: 2026/10/12
   - **Conference Date**: 2026/12/5

We aim to be an accessible, technical, and community-driven conference for
novice to advanced engineers alike. If you have hands-on experience applying AI
agents to real-world engineering challenges, innovative projects, or open-source
tools to share, we would be delighted to hear how you tackle complex problems
and grow a stronger open-source community together.

If you have any questions or need assistance with your proposal, please do not
hesitate to contact us at [contact@sciwork.dev](mailto:contact@sciwork.dev).

We look forward to receiving your submissions and your participation in sciwork 2026!
